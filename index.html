About us

Diana's Tailoring Services offers clothing repair, alterations, and made-to-order sewing. Customers seek us out for our tailoring work, from our own community and from other areas.

Every piece gets hands-on attention, so we can only take a limited number of orders at a time. Even in our busiest seasons we handle around 5 to 7 orders a day. That's how we make sure each garment gets the care it deserves.

Our Services

Diana's Tailoring Services offers clothing repair, alterations, custom tailoring and made-to-order sewing, for customers from our own community and from other areas.

Every piece gets hands-on attention, which is why we take a limited number of orders at a time. Whether you need a torn seam mended, a favorite outfit resized, or a garment made around your own measurements, each job is done with care.
